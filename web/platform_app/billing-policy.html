Billing, Cancellation & Refunds

How paid access, renewal cancellation and refund-review requests work. Last updated 3 October 2026.

Server-priced checkout

The product, price, currency and duration presented by SignalRankAI are resolved by the server and verified against Paystack transaction metadata. Browser-supplied amounts are not trusted.

Renewal

Where a subscription is configured to renew automatically, the account shows that state. You can use the account cancellation control to disable auto-renew. Current paid access normally remains until the end of the already-paid period.

Cancellation

Turning off auto-renew prevents future renewal when the provider cancellation is confirmed. Cancellation does not itself issue a refund for the current period. If provider cancellation cannot be confirmed, SignalRankAI opens a follow-up support path.

Refund review

Refunds are not automatic. Use the authenticated refund-review form with a payment reference belonging to your account. The request is reviewed against the payment record, service supplied, provider status, applicable terms and law before any refund decision or provider action.

Receipts and disputes

Keep the payment reference and receipt. If a charge appears incorrect or duplicated, contact Billing support before sharing sensitive financial information. Never send full card credentials, PINs or OTPs.

Provider timing

Settlement, reversals and card/bank posting times can depend on Paystack and the relevant financial institution. SignalRankAI cannot promise an external bank's posting time.
