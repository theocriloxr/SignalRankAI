Cookie Policy

SignalRankAI separates necessary security/session storage from optional marketing choices. Last updated 3 October 2026.

Necessary storage

Secure sessions, CSRF protection, refresh/access tokens, theme/navigation preferences and fraud/security controls may require cookies or local storage. These are used to provide the service you request.

Optional analytics

If optional analytics is introduced on the public app, it must be disclosed and, where required, withheld until consent is obtained. Sensitive trading, account, credential and support content should not be intentionally sent to optional analytics.

Marketing

Marketing consent is not required to create or use an account. SignalRankAI should not load advertising or cross-site marketing technology merely because a user accepts platform terms.

Third-party checkout and integrations

When you intentionally open Paystack, Telegram, a broker or another external provider, that provider may use its own cookies or storage under its own policy.

The current app uses necessary browser storage for authentication and preferences. Any future non-essential tracking should be added only with an explicit category and consent control where legally required.
