Risk Disclosure

Important limitations before using market signals, paper trading, broker connections or execution features.

Loss risk

Trading and leveraged products can lose money quickly. Stop losses may slip, gap or fail to fill at the displayed level in fast, illiquid or disrupted markets.

Model risk

Scores, probabilities and AI/model outputs are estimates, can be wrong and may behave differently when market regimes, data quality or provider conditions change.

Data and execution risk

Delayed, stale, incomplete or conflicting data can affect analysis. Broker, exchange, network or provider failures can delay orders, fills, cancellations, reconciliations or account state.

Backtest and paper limits

Backtests and simulations can suffer from overfitting, survivorship bias, unrealistic fills and hindsight effects. Paper results do not reproduce every live spread, slippage, latency, rejection, fee or behavioural factor.

Prop and broker rules

Your broker or prop firm may impose leverage, instrument, news, holding-period, automation, copy-trading, drawdown or account-sharing restrictions. SignalRankAI cannot waive those rules.

No guarantee

No plan, signal, model confidence, historical win rate, certification label or risk control eliminates the possibility of loss. Only you can decide whether a trade is appropriate for your circumstances.

Execution terms are separate. General account terms do not enable live or automated execution. Any execution workflow must retain its own explicit risk acceptance and safety gates.
