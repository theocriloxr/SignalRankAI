# Phase 4 Pass 8 — Evidence and Performance Truth

Date: 2026-07-19  
Status: Foundation complete; no profitability claim.

`ml.evidence` and `engine.performance_truth` provide deterministic manifests,
dataset hashes, cost/fill assumptions, provenance-separated metrics, Wilson
confidence bounds, purged walk-forward index splits, and conservative
candidate-promotion decisions. A candidate requires out-of-sample sample size,
positive WFO folds, positive expectancy, profit factor, drawdown limits, and
human approval. Backtest, shadow, paper, manual, and live results remain
distinct. Identical manifests hash identically; creation time is metadata only.

The system does not claim a 60% win rate. Historical and live evidence must be
supplied before any promotion or public performance statement.
